AuraRead Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026
Effective Date: May 16, 2026

📱 Introduction

AuraRead ("we", "our", or "us") is a local e-book reader. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the AuraRead application (the "App", Bundle ID: com.aura.reader).

Important:

  • We do not actively collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Books you import, reading progress, and statistics are stored on your device by default. We do not operate servers to host your library.
  • To improve the App, and only when enabled (you can turn this off in Settings), we use Google Firebase Analytics to collect de-identified usage statistics as described below. We do not upload book text, chapter content, titles, authors, or file names via Firebase.

🔍 Information We Collect

1. Personal Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask you to provide:

  • ❌ Name
  • ❌ Email address
  • ❌ Phone number
  • ❌ Precise location
  • ❌ Contacts or photo library (except when you voluntarily import books via the system file picker)

Note: If you leave Analytics enabled in Settings, Firebase Analytics may automatically collect de-identified technical information (such as device identifiers, IP address, and usage data) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can disable this at any time under Settings → Enable Analytics.

2. Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data only on your device and does not upload it to our servers:

Data Type Purpose Storage Location
Book files Reading TXT, EPUB, MOBI, etc. App sandbox Documents/Books
Library metadata Title, cover, progress, chapters Local Core Data
Reading sessions Reading time statistics, heatmap Local Core Data
Reading settings Font, theme, page-turn animation, etc. Local Core Data / UserDefaults
App settings Appearance, language, etc. Local UserDefaults

Note: This data is created or imported by you. Except for Web Import over your local network (described below), we do not retrieve this data from your device.

🛠 Technologies and Permissions

1. File Import (Files / Share / Open In)

You may import e-books via the system file picker, Share sheet, or "Open in AuraRead". Imported files are saved in the App sandbox for on-device reading only.

2. Web Import (Local Network)

Under Settings → Web Import, the App can start a local HTTP server on your iPhone or iPad (using the open-source GCDWebServer library) so you can upload books from a computer browser on the same Wi‑Fi.

Item Details
Network scope Your local network only; no connection to our cloud servers
Data flow Uploaded files are written directly to your on-device library; we do not relay them
System permissions Local Network permission (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription); Bonjour service type _http._tcp
Usage note The server runs only while Web Import is enabled and the App stays in the foreground; it stops when you leave that screen

Data flow: Transfers occur only between your device and your computer on your LAN; we cannot access it.

3. Open-Source Components (On-Device Processing)

The App uses these third-party libraries to process content locally; they do not send your library to the developers' servers:

  • GCDWebServer: LAN web upload
  • ZIPFoundation: EPUB and other archive parsing
  • Kanna: HTML/XML parsing

4. Features Not Currently Offered

As of the effective date of this policy, the App does not provide or enable:

  • ❌ iCloud sync (planned for a future version, not yet available)
  • ❌ Account sign-in or cloud library
  • ❌ Marketing push notifications
  • ❌ Built-in bookstore or content distribution

The App does not include:

  • ❌ Third-party advertising SDKs
  • ❌ Standalone crash reporting SDKs (e.g., Crashlytics; errors may be logged via Firebase Analytics only)
  • ❌ Social media SDKs
  • ❌ Remote library or sync services operated by us

📖 Content You Import

The App does not provide copyrighted e-books. All books are imported by you. You must ensure you have the legal right to use imported content. We are not responsible for user-imported content and cannot access or review your on-device library.

🔒 Data Security

  1. Device security: Protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.
  2. Sandbox isolation: App data is stored in the iOS sandbox; other apps cannot read it directly.
  3. Local Web Import: Listens on your LAN only when you enable it; use on trusted Wi‑Fi and turn it off when finished.
  4. iCloud Backup: If enabled, backup data is encrypted by Apple.

👶 Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose reader and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not show third-party ads and does not collect children's names or contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us using the details below.

🌍 International Data Transfers

  • Library and reading data: Stored on your device by default; we do not transfer it internationally.
  • Firebase Analytics (if enabled): data may be transferred to Google's servers outside your country, subject to Google's privacy policy and security measures.
  • Web Import: Transfers occur only on your local network, not through our servers.
  • Apple services (e.g., iCloud Backup, App Store): Governed by Apple's policies and may involve servers outside your country.

📝 Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, China's Personal Information Protection Law), you may have the following rights:

1. Right to Access

You can view your library, reading progress, and statistics in the App.

2. Right to Erasure

  • Delete individual books from the bookshelf.
  • Delete all data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in system Settings.

3. Right to Withdraw Consent / Opt Out of Analytics

You can disable Firebase Analytics at any time under Settings → Enable Analytics. You can also disable Local Network access for AuraRead under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Web Import will not work, but local reading of imported books is unaffected.

4. Right to Data Portability

Imported book files are stored in the App sandbox; you may back them up via file sharing or other system features where available.

For material changes, we may notify you via in-app notice or App Store release notes. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy; if you disagree, please stop using the App and uninstall it.
