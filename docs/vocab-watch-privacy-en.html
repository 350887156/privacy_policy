拾词记 (Shiciji) Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026
Effective Date: May 22, 2026

📱 Introduction

拾词记 / Shiciji ("we", "our", or "us") is a local-first English vocabulary learning app for iPhone and Apple Watch. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the 拾词记 application (the "App").

App identifiers:

  • iPhone app Bundle ID: com.lajiaoyang.vocabulary
  • Apple Watch companion Bundle ID: com.lajiaoyang.vocabulary.watchkitapp
  • Widget / Live Activity extension: com.lajiaoyang.vocabulary.widgets

Important:

  • We do not require account registration and do not actively collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your study progress, review schedules, deck selection, and mistake records are stored on your devices by default (and may sync between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch via Apple frameworks). We do not operate servers to host your learning history.
  • To improve the App, Release builds distributed via the App Store may use Google Firebase Analytics to collect de-identified usage statistics as described below. We do not upload specific word spellings, full definitions, example sentences, or your personal study logs through Firebase.
  • Debug builds (e.g., when running from Xcode) do not enable Firebase Analytics.

🔍 Information We Collect

1. Personal Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask you to provide:

  • ❌ Name
  • ❌ Email address
  • ❌ Phone number
  • ❌ Precise location
  • ❌ Contacts, photo library, or microphone recordings (the App does not use voice input for vocabulary study)

Note: When Firebase Analytics is enabled in Release builds, Firebase may automatically collect de-identified technical information (such as device identifiers, IP address, and usage data) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Google acts as an independent processor for that data.

2. Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data primarily on your device and does not upload it to our own servers:

Note: Study data is generated by your use of the App; dictionary content is bundled from open-source datasets, not files you upload.

🛠 Technologies and Permissions

1. Apple Watch and iPhone Sync

The App includes an Apple Watch companion that uses Apple's WatchConnectivity and App Group (group.com.lajiaoyang.vocabulary) to sync review queues, quiz results, and related study state between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch.

Item Details
Data flow Between your personal devices only; not relayed through servers we operate
Requirements Install the watch app via the system Watch app; pairing is managed by Apple
Your control You may stop syncing, remove the watch app, or unpair devices at any time

2. Pronunciation (System TTS)

The App uses Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer for on-device text-to-speech. Quality depends on voice packs installed on your device. We do not send pronunciation requests to our own servers.

3. Lock Screen Live Activity & Widgets

Where supported, the App may show today's study progress (e.g., completed count, due reviews) via ActivityKit and WidgetKit. Data comes from the on-device App Group and is not sent to our servers.

4. Open-Source Dictionary Data

Word lists are derived from open-source projects such as ECDICT and CC-CEDICT (full attributions under Settings → Dictionary & Licenses). Content is loaded locally; no network is required for study after the bundled library is available on device (first launch may show a local import progress).

5. Features Not Currently Offered

As of the effective date of this policy, the App does not provide or enable:

  • ❌ Account sign-in or cloud study profiles
  • ❌ Remote study-history sync operated by us
  • ❌ Third-party advertising SDKs
  • ❌ Standalone crash reporting SDKs (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics)
  • ❌ Marketing push notifications for advertising

📖 Dictionary Content

Exam word lists and examples come from open-source dictionary datasets (filtered and converted for in-app use). We do not guarantee academic completeness of third-party source data. The App is intended for personal study; please comply with applicable open-source licenses (see in-app notices). We cannot access your full on-device study archive and do not review how you use dictionary entries.

🔒 Data Security

  1. Device security: Protect your iPhone and Apple Watch with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.
  2. Sandbox isolation: App data is stored in the iOS / watchOS sandbox; other apps cannot read it directly.
  3. Paired devices: Watch sync occurs only between devices you have paired; keep your Apple devices secure.
  4. iCloud Backup: If enabled, backup data is encrypted by Apple.

👶 Children's Privacy

The App is a general vocabulary learning tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not show third-party ads and does not collect children's names or contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us using the details below.

🌍 International Data Transfers

  • Study and dictionary usage data: Stored on your devices (and paired Watch) by default; we do not transfer it to our own servers internationally.
  • Firebase Analytics (Release builds): data may be transferred to Google's servers outside your country, subject to Google's privacy policy and security measures.
  • Apple services (e.g., iCloud Backup, App Store, Watch pairing): Governed by Apple's policies and may involve servers outside your country.

📝 Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, China's Personal Information Protection Law), you may have the following rights:

1. Right to Access

You can view study progress, statistics, decks, and settings in the App.

2. Right to Erasure

  • Reset progress: switch decks or use in-app reset options (if available), or uninstall the App.
  • Delete all data: uninstall the iPhone app and the Watch companion, or clear app data in system Settings.

3. Right to Restrict Processing / Opt Out of Analytics

Release builds currently enable Firebase Analytics; Debug builds do not. To request restriction, contact us; a future version may add an in-app disable option.

4. Right to Data Portability

v1.0 does not offer one-tap export of your study archive; rely on system backup or future export features if added.
